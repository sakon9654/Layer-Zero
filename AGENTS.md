# Layer Zero Novel — Writing Instructions

This repository is the source of truth for the Layer Zero novel.

## Canon priority

Use information in this order when sources disagree:

1. Explicit author decisions in the current task
2. Files marked `author-only-canon`
3. Files marked `established-canon`
4. Approved story chapters
5. Draft outlines and planning notes
6. Imported conversations under `sources/`

Never silently resolve a Canon conflict. Report the conflicting files and ask the author which version should prevail.

## Before writing or revising story content

1. Read `canon/INDEX.md`.
2. Read the relevant world, organization, character, and anomaly files.
3. Check the master timeline and continuity notes.
4. Check the current Arc outline and existing chapters.
5. Keep hidden Canon out of character knowledge and reader-facing prose until the planned reveal.

## Canon boundaries

- `canon/` contains authoritative setting information.
- `canon/hidden/` is author-only knowledge and may contain spoilers.
- `story/` contains outlines and manuscript content; status metadata determines whether it is a draft or approved.
- `sources/` preserves provenance. Its contents are references, not Canon by themselves.
- New facts introduced while drafting are proposals until the author approves them.

## Style

- Write novel prose in Thai.
- Preserve established English terms for organizations, classifications, and anomalies when appropriate.
- Blend mystery, investigation, supernatural horror, cosmic horror, bureaucratic horror, action, and survival.
- Keep the contemporary setting grounded.
- Supernatural phenomena may have observable patterns, but their full nature can remain unknown.
- Do not introduce a conventional magic system without author approval.

## Character continuity

Characters must retain established personalities, knowledge, motivations, and limitations. Do not reveal hidden information prematurely.
